# Simulation results

_Generated by `python -m sim.run --minutes 60`. 50 agents, 60 simulated minutes per run, wrap-up 6-14s, same random seed for every run._

- **util**: share of logged-in agent time spent talking to a borrower.
- **waiting**: share of logged-in time AVAILABLE, RESERVED or DIALING (not talking, not in wrap-up).
- **abandoned**: borrower answered and no agent was connected (the compliance metric).
- **safety decisions**: one per pacing tick (1s). REJECT here almost always means "the pacer wanted more, but the in-flight ceiling is already reached".

Scenarios: **A** 20% answer, 120s talk; **B** 50% answer, 90s talk; **C** 70% answer, 180s talk; **D** answer 30%->70%->10%->45% every 15m; talk 90->180->60s every 20m.
Provider A: fast and reliable. Provider B: 2-6s setup, 3% start timeouts, 2% rejects, 15% duplicate webhooks, 0.2-3s webhook delay, 3% of webhooks 8-30s late (reordered).
